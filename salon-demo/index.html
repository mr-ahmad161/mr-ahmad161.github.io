The pain every salon owner already knows

Researched from how salons actually lose clients — not from a features list.

01

The phone rings mid-appointment

Your hands are in someone's hair. You can't stop a colour service to answer — so the call goes to voicemail, and most callers never leave one. They just book the next salon on Google.

02

Clients decide after hours

People plan their hair at 9 PM on the sofa, not at 11 AM at your front desk. If nobody answers in the evening, the booking goes to whichever salon replies first — usually a competitor.

03

No-shows burn the chair twice

One forgotten appointment is an empty chair you can't refill at the last minute — you lose the service fee and the walk-in you turned away. No reminder went out because the front desk was slammed.
